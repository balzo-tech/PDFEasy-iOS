RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

Agreement no. 2026/0418 — executed in Boston, Massachusetts

This agreement is entered into between Whitfield Property Holdings LLC, having its registered office at 74 Ashford Lane, Boston, MA 02116 (the "Landlord"), and Daniel R. Marsh, holder of driver's licence S4419820 (the "Tenant").

1. Premises

The Landlord lets to the Tenant the apartment located at 122 Bellamy Street, unit 4B, Boston, MA 02118, comprising four rooms, a kitchen and two bathrooms, with a floor area of 96 square metres, together with one assigned parking space in the basement garage.

2. Term

The lease runs for four years, commencing on 1 September 2026 and expiring on 31 August 2030. It renews automatically for a further four years unless notice of termination is served in accordance with clause 6.

3. Rent

The rent is $1,200 per month, payable in advance by the fifth day of each month by bank transfer to the account indicated by the Landlord. The rent is adjusted annually by 75% of the change in the consumer price index published for the preceding twelve months.

4. Security deposit

On signature the Tenant pays a security deposit of $2,400, equal to two months' rent. The deposit bears interest at the statutory rate and is returned within thirty days of the handover of the keys, less any sums owed for damage beyond ordinary wear and tear.

5. Utilities and charges

Water, heating and building maintenance charges are borne by the Tenant and settled on an actual-cost basis at the end of each calendar year. Electricity and internet are contracted directly by the Tenant in the Tenant's own name.

6. Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement by giving three months' written notice sent by registered letter with return receipt. The Tenant may withdraw at any time on the same notice where serious grounds so require, without penalty.

7. Condition of the premises

The Tenant takes the premises in the condition recorded in the inventory annexed hereto and undertakes to return them in the same condition, ordinary wear and tear excepted. Alterations to the structure require the Landlord's prior written consent.

8. Governing law

Any matter not expressly regulated herein is governed by the residential tenancy law of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts. Disputes are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Suffolk County.
