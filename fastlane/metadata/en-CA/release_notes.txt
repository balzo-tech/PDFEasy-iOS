MAKE A MEME
Pick a picture — one of the templates everybody already knows, or a photo of your own — and write on it. Put the words where the joke needs them: drag them, pinch them bigger or smaller, choose the face (Impact is in there), the size, the colour and the alignment. Then save it to your photos or send it to someone.

Also new: the image editor. Crop a photo, turn it, straighten it and set the brightness, the contrast and the colour before it becomes a PDF.

And the app now speaks Dutch.
