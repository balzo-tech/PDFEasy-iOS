STRAIGHT PAGES, REAL NAMES, AND SEARCH THAT FINDS THINGS
Crooked photographs become straight pages on their own: when the writing is on its side, the app notices and turns it.

A document made of photos is no longer called "File-09-17-2026". It takes the title written on it, and you can search for the words inside it, not only for its name.

The shortcuts at the top of Tools are now the ones you actually use.
