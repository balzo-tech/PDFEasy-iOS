This is the largest update PDF Pro has had. Almost every part of the app was rebuilt.

A SCANNER OF OUR OWN
Camera scanning is now built in: the outline follows the page, the shutter fires when your hand settles, and scans land in their own tab.

AN ARCHIVE THAT CAN BE SEARCHED
Folders and tags, and search that looks inside the text of your documents instead of only at their names. It all syncs through your iCloud account.

AN EDITOR THAT KEEPS UP
Long documents and scans open immediately and stay smooth while you scroll — pages are drawn as you reach them. One tool list, a page bar that follows the document, and every tool on its own screen instead of a stack of sheets.

MORE TOOLS
Rotate a whole document, compare two PDFs, compress with presets that say what you are trading away, redact, read aloud, and let the app suggest a file name from the document itself.

REDESIGNED
A new look throughout, a layout made for iPad, a Home Screen widget, and Siri and Shortcuts support.

ASK YOUR DOCUMENT
The chat was rebuilt: clearer answers, suggested questions, and formatting that is actually readable.

NOW IN SPANISH
Alongside English and Italian.

Plus faster launches, steadier iCloud sync, and a long list of fixes.
