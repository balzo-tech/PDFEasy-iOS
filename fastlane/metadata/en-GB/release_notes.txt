OPEN SIGNED DOCUMENTS (.p7m)
The file your bank, your notary or the public administration sends you: iOS has no viewer for it, Mail shows an unknown attachment, and every guide on the subject ends with "use a computer". PDF PRO now takes the envelope off and the PDF opens like any other document - from the file picker, from Mail, or with "Copy to PDF PRO". Before it opens, it shows you who the envelope names as its signer.

Recognises .p7m, .m7m and .p7s.
