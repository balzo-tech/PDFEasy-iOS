PASSPORT PHOTOS, FROM YOUR PHONE
The photo for a passport, an ID card or a driving licence, in your country's own measurements: 35 × 45 mm in the UK and Europe, 26 × 32 in Spain, square in the United States. The app finds the face, measures the head from the chin to the top of the hair and crops it for you. Then it tells you what is wrong before you go and print: head tilted, eyes half shut, a shadow down one side of the face, an uneven background. Pick a white, grey or light blue backdrop, move the frame if you want to, and get a 10 × 15 cm sheet with six photos to cut out.

Also new: a tool that removes the background from a photo.
